FloorVote themes

Eight looks for FloorVote, each a set of colors, typefaces, and measures that a team or a person could pick, plus three layouts that work with any of them. The District theme shows how a state could ship its own flag and colors. Bill titles are real youth justice bills from DC, Maryland, and Virginia, and Maryland's hearing dates are real. Statuses, other dates, votes, names, and comments are sample data for Tuesday, February 3, 2026.

A theme is data

Each theme is one small file: about 30 named colors for light and dark, up to six typefaces, a few measures (corner radius, row spacing, density), and an optional flag. The picker swaps these values. It never changes what a screen shows or where things sit.

Who picks

An admin sets the team's default theme. Each person can keep it or choose their own, and separately choose System, Light, or Dark. The Appearance screen above sketches the setting.

Layouts are separate

Three of these themes were first proposed together with a new layout. Here they come apart: the split pane, the lead story, and the team room are product decisions to test on their own, and every theme can dress them.
